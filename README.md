## 📌 About This Repository

Welcome to my **8 Week SQL Challenge** repository!

This repository documents my journey through the [8 Week SQL Challenge](https://8weeksqlchallenge.com/) created by **Danny Ma (Data With Danny)**.

As an aspiring **Data Analyst / Data Scientist**, I am using this challenge to strengthen my SQL proficiency, improve analytical thinking, and practice translating business questions into meaningful, data-driven insights.

Rather than simply writing queries, my goal is to understand the underlying business problems, explore the datasets, develop efficient SQL solutions, and communicate actionable findings.

Each case study includes SQL solutions, explanations of key techniques, and business insights where applicable.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- **SQL Proficiency:** Strengthen fundamental and advanced SQL querying skills.
- **Analytical Thinking:** Translate real-world business questions into structured analytical approaches.
- **Data Exploration:** Explore relational datasets and identify meaningful patterns.
- **Data Transformation:** Practice joins, aggregations, CTEs, window functions, and conditional logic.
- **Business Understanding:** Connect SQL results with business performance and decision-making.
- **Portfolio Development:** Document solutions and communicate insights through a well-structured GitHub repository.

## 🍜 Featured Case Study — Week 01: Danny's Diner

**Status: Completed | Database: PostgreSQL | 10 Questions + 2 Bonus Challenges**

The first case study explores customer purchasing behavior at Danny's Diner, a small Japanese restaurant seeking to better understand its customers and loyalty program.

### Business Questions Explored

- How much does each customer spend?
- How frequently do customers visit the restaurant?
- Which menu items are the most popular?
- What are customers' purchasing patterns before and after joining the membership program?
- How does the loyalty points system reward customer spending?

### Key Results

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | **$186** |
| Total Customers | **3** |
| Most Purchased Item | **Ramen (8 purchases)** |
| Highest-Spending Customer | **Customer A ($76)** |
| Main Questions Completed | **10 / 10** |
| Bonus Questions | **2 solutions documented** |

## 🔍 My Analytical Approach

I follow a structured approach to solving each SQL case study:

**1. Understand the Business Problem**

Identify the analytical objective, relevant business metrics, and expected output.

**2. Explore the Data**

Review database schemas, table relationships, column definitions, and data characteristics.

**3. Develop SQL Solutions**

Break down complex questions into logical steps and implement SQL queries using appropriate techniques.

**4. Validate Results**

Execute queries, verify calculations, investigate unexpected results, and refine query logic.

**5. Extract Business Insights**

Interpret query outputs and identify patterns, opportunities, and potential business implications.

**6. Document & Reflect**

Organize solutions, explain important techniques, and summarize key learning outcomes for future reference.
